**ScholarTrace** is an academic research integrity platform that continuously monitors references used in registered research papers and detects changes such as retractions and other critical status updates.

It connects researchers and academic institutions, allowing universities to track registered research, identify affected publications, and notify researchers when a cited source becomes problematic.

The system is designed around continuous monitoring, reliable external data synchronization, impact analysis, role-based access control, and an auditable history of research and citation status changes.
